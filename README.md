# Customer Value & Retention Strategy (E-commerce Activewear)

## Overview
This project analyses customer purchasing behaviour for a premium e-commerce activewear brand (Rooster) to identify the key drivers of customer value and retention. The analysis combines descriptive analytics, segmentation, and predictive modelling to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem
While Rooster successfully attracts customers, most customers do not return after their first purchase. This low repeat purchase rate limits long-term revenue growth. The goal is to identify how to increase customer lifetime value through retention strategies.

## Key Questions
- What are the patterns in customer spending and repeat purchases?
- Which customers contribute most to revenue?
- What factors drive customer value?

## Dataset
Transactional data across 5 tables:
- Customers
- Orders
- Products
- Orderlines
- Metadata

> Note: Dataset is not included due to confidentiality. A sample or structure can be recreated.

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and quality assessment
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Customer segmentation
- Predictive modelling (Linear Regression)

## Key Insights
- Most customers make only **one purchase (avg = 1.29 orders)** → low retention
- Revenue is concentrated among a **small group of high-value customers**
- **Repeat purchases significantly increase total spending**
- Sales show **strong seasonality** (peak in June and December)
- **Multi-pack products** generate the highest revenue

## Business Recommendations
1. Implement a **loyalty program** to increase repeat purchases
2. Target **high-value customers** with personalised offers
3. Use **discounts strategically** during low-demand months
4. Focus on **bundle products** (multi-pack strategy)
5. Improve **data quality (SKU tracking)** for inventory management
