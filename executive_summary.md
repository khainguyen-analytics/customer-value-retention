# Executive Summary

## Problem
Rooster faces low customer retention, with most customers making only one purchase, limiting long-term revenue growth.

## Approach
The analysis used:
- Descriptive analytics
- Customer segmentation
- Predictive modelling (regression)

## Key Findings
- Repeat purchases strongly drive customer value
- Revenue is concentrated among high-value customers
- Seasonal trends affect sales performance
- Bundle products generate the most revenue

## Recommendations
- Implement loyalty programs to improve retention
- Target high-value customers with personalised marketing
- Use discounts strategically during low-demand periods
- Focus on bundle product strategies

## Impact
Improving customer retention can significantly increase customer lifetime value and overall revenue.
